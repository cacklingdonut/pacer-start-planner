How it works

Each pace group is modeled as running a perfectly even split from the moment it crosses the start line. Given your corral-based or manually input start time, we find which groups are catchable, where to join and leave each one, and your pace between them. The suggested plan maximizes time spent with pacers subject to your goal time and your ±band pace range.

  • Corral start times and pacer crossings are estimates for the Wine & Dine half — refine them in the assumptions panel once actual results are published.
  • "Paced" time means staying within sight of the group's sign — a plan is a guide, not a guarantee.
